## What Kind of Game Is War Footing?

The title is literal. War Footing is about putting a nation onto a war footing — mobilizing industry, budgets, and political capital before combat ever starts. You are not a battlefield commander micro-managing tanks. You are the Minister of Defense, and your job is procurement, force structure, and politics.

The structure is unusual for the genre, so it helps to see it laid out.

| System | What You Actually Do | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Procurement | Source equipment from foreign suppliers or your own domestic industry | Cost, capability, and availability each carry trade-offs |
| Force structure | Raise and outfit units from company to brigade level | Readiness and doctrine fit decide who wins |
| Politics | Manage diplomatic obligations and domestic political stability | Stability keeps the budget flowing; ignore it and funding dries up |
| Combat | Battles resolve at the operational level, sector by sector, week by week | Outcomes reflect decisions you made before the war started |

Each turn represents one week. You spend those weeks acquiring gear, training units, and assigning them to positions. When the invasion finally comes, the army you built is the army you fight with — there is no last-minute scramble to fix a bad procurement plan.

The game ships with eight scenarios, each with different starting conditions, resource constraints, and enemy configurations. There are more than 180 equipment types to consider, spanning small arms, armor, artillery, aircraft, and air defense systems. That number sounds impressive, but the real design tension is that almost everything is a trade-off — the cheapest option is rarely the best one, and the best one is rarely available in the quantity you need.

The store page's user-defined tags point toward a late-Cold-War-flavored, alternate-history setting, with "1980s," "1990s," and "Cold War" appearing prominently. The developers also note that the conflict is fictional and that violence is abstract rather than graphically described.
