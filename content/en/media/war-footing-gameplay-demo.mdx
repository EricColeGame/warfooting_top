## What Is War Footing?

War Footing is a strategy game about running a modern military campaign — not just winning battles, but sustaining them. You manage equipment inventories, production queues, airbase capacity, maintenance hangars, and events that shift the strategic picture turn by turn.

Its board game DNA shows. Systems are legible, numbers matter, and every decision has a paper-trail logic to it: what you build, what you upgrade, and what you send forward.

The digital version keeps that structure while adding things a tabletop game cannot easily do — automated combat resolution, filterable industry screens, and scenario scripting that changes enemy strength.

Because the game is still in active development, some systems are still moving. That is normal for a pre-demo build, and it is worth keeping in mind when reading patch notes.

### What These Changes Mean in Practice

A few of these adjustments are more significant than they look. Making armor more decisive in penetration calculations means armored vehicles should survive longer and contribute more, which pushes players toward protecting them rather than treating them as disposable.

Tying vehicle losses more strongly to unit readiness creates a feedback loop: losing tanks and transports degrades the formation that lost them, not just the equipment count. That makes withdrawal a real tactical option rather than a failure state.

Finally, the one-click equipment upgrade and the sortable industry view are the kind of quality-of-life features that matter enormously in a game with deep inventories. They reduce the busywork between battles.

## Practical Tips for Your First War Footing Gameplay Demo Session

Knowing the patch notes in advance gives you a real edge. These tips are drawn directly from what the recent builds changed.

| Situation | Recommended Approach | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Your infantry keep melting in assaults | Pair them with armor before pushing | Armor presence reduces infantry casualties in the current combat model |

| You keep running out of equipment mid-campaign | Use the one-click upgrade and watch the production queue screen | Upgrading to best available and tracking turns remaining prevents gaps |

| You are playing on a laptop screen | Update to the latest build before starting | Recent fixes specifically target smaller displays |

### Habits That Pay Off

- **Think in turns, not battles.** Production queues, readiness recovery, and event timing all resolve on a turn cadence.
- **Treat trucks as combat multipliers.** With medevac weighting shifted toward ground transport, logistics vehicles do more than move supplies.
- **Audit your inventory between engagements.** The equipment assignment fix in recent builds means inventory items should now be assignable where they previously were not.
- **Check airbase capacity rules.** Helicopter capacity limits were relaxed, so your air planning has more room than older builds allowed.
- **Expect events to matter.** Multiple updates added and rebalanced events, which suggests they are a meaningful lever rather than flavor.

### What to Watch After the Demo

Once you have played a few sessions, the most useful thing you can do is note where the balance feels off — particularly around armor survivability and air-to-air outcomes, since both were recently retuned. That kind of specific feedback is what demo periods are for.

## FAQ

**When does the War Footing gameplay demo release?**
The developer's June update tentatively targeted mid-July, with the caveat that the build still needed playtesting and balance work. Because that window was described as tentative, the safest approach is to follow the Steam page for confirmation rather than treating any single date as locked in.

**Is the War Footing gameplay demo free?**
The dev update does not state pricing. Steam demos are typically free to download, but the store page is the authoritative source for how this particular demo will be distributed.

**Do I need to have played the board game version?**
No. The digital release is a standalone strategy game. Familiarity with the board game concept may help you read the systems faster, but the interface, combat resolution, and scenario setup are built for a PC audience.

**What makes the War Footing gameplay demo different from other wargames?**
The emphasis on sustainment rather than pure tactics. Maintenance hangars, airbase capacity, production queues, and equipment upgrades all sit alongside combat, which means the War Footing gameplay demo asks you to manage an ongoing war effort rather than a single battle.
